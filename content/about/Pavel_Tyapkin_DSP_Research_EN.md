## Professional Summary

Communications and digital signal processing engineer with approximately **10 years of engineering experience** in satellite communications, navigation receivers, SDR, wireless systems, and embedded signal-processing platforms. Combines **MATLAB-based algorithm modeling and experimental validation** with practical implementation and debugging on **FPGA/SoC hardware**.

Interested in communications R&D roles that connect signal processing theory with working radio hardware.
