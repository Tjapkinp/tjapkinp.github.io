## Professional Profile

FPGA and digital signal processing engineer with approximately 10 years of hands-on experience in communication systems, real-time signal processing, SDR, and embedded platforms. Experienced in developing and integrating RTL for FPGA/SoC devices, optimizing FPGA designs for timing and resource utilization, implementing hardware interfaces, and validating communication algorithms through simulation and experimental testbeds.

Combines industrial engineering and technical coordination experience with applied research in interference mitigation, blind source separation, and deep-space communications. Interested in FPGA/DSP engineering, SDR and wireless PHY development, and research-oriented roles combining MATLAB-based algorithms with hardware implementation.
